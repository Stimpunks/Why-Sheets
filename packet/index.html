If this does not work — an old browser, a blocked script, a device that will not cooperate — every sheet still has its own PDF on its own page, and printing them one at a time gets you the same paper. The packet saves you the assembly, not the argument.

Nothing is sent anywhere. This page downloads the same PDFs anyone can download, joins them in your browser, and hands you the result. There is no account, no upload, no analytics, and no record of which sheets you picked. What you are assembling is nobody else's business.
